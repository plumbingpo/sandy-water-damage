Roof Leak Water Damage in Sandy: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. Often the attic has been damp for some time before any brown mark reaches the room below. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

We are a water damage crew, not a roofing company. Our job is the inside of the house: pulling out soaked insulation, drying the framing and drywall, treating for mold and putting the ceiling back together. If rain is still coming in, we can tarp the roof as a temporary cover, or work alongside the roofer you choose so the leak is fixed before we close anything up.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsTurn off the breaker for any light, fan or outlet that has water running through or near it, then keep your hands off it.
  2. Contain the WaterFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Lay towels or plastic sheeting down to protect the floor.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofDo not climb onto a wet roof or crawl through a dark attic. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Document What You SeeBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.
The Usual Culprits

Why This Happens

  • Shingles that are cracked, curled or blown off by wind, exposing the underlayment.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • A skylight whose curb or seal has dried and cracked, so rain gets past it.
  • Fasteners working loose over time, so water follows the nail shank into the attic.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
If It Is Left

Where the Damage Spreads

Ceiling Drywall

Staining, sagging and loss of strength follow when drywall is not dried. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Rafters and Decking

Repeated wetting makes decking, trusses and rafters turn dark, swell up and go soft. Rot in these members weakens the structure that holds the roof up.

Lights and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Mold in the Attic

A dark, warm attic with damp wood and paper-faced drywall suits mold well. Growth can begin within 24 to 48 hours and spread across the underside of the decking.

Will Insurance Pay?

Does Insurance Cover It?

Homeowners policies often cover interior water damage when a roof leak is sudden and accidental, such as wind tearing off shingles or a limb puncturing the roof. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

What It Costs

What Affects the Cost

  • Mold, if present, and the number of days or weeks water was getting in.
  • The amount of ceiling and wall drywall that needs to be cut out.
  • The ceiling texture and paint that the repair has to match.
  • Damage beyond the ceiling, such as wet floors, wall cavities or furniture.

Our Process

What Happens After You Call

1

Trace the Leak Path

Using thermal imaging and moisture meters, our team checks wall cavities, ceilings and the attic and charts the whole route the water took, even where the room below shows nothing.

2

Cover and Protect

An exposed roof gets covered with a temporary tarp, or we line things up with your roofing contractor. Floors and belongings below the wet ceiling are covered as well.

3

Tear Out and Dry

Unsalvageable drywall and insulation come out, and dehumidifiers and air movers go into the attic space and each room under it, with readings taken until the framing has dried.

4

Sanitize and Repair

Exposed wood gets an antimicrobial treatment where needed. Once the roof repair is done, we replace insulation, hang and finish new drywall, then prime and paint to match the ceiling.
